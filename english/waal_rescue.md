# Baby Whale Rescued at Marine Reserve
Last Saturday, a baby whale was rescued at the Marine Reserve of Moss Beach. Mike, a 14-year-old volunteer working at Moss Beach, initiated the rescue. According to reports, he first spotted the stranded whale lying on the beach and called Hailey for help. Hailey and her coworkers then drove to the area where the whale was spotted. The baby whale was lying in pain, strangled by a harsh fishing rope, but rescue was near. Karen, one of Hailey's employees, made a quick decision and carefully started to cut the whale free from the rope. The baby whale, half dead with smears of blood all over its body after days of suffering, slowly started to crawl back into the ocean. It was a heartbreaking moment that brought tears of joy to the eyes of Hailey and her coworkers.
