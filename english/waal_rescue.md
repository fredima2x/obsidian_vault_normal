# Baby Whale Rescued at Marine Reserve
Last Saturday, a Baby Whale was Rescued at the Marine Reserve of Moss Beach. A 14 year old Volunteer, working at Moss Beach initiated the Rescue. According to reports, Mike first spotted the strandet whale laying on the beach and called Hailey for help. Hailey and their Coworkers than drove to the Area the Whale was spotted. The baby Whale was lying in pain, strangeled by a harsh fishing rope, but rescue was near. Karen, one of Haileys employees, made a fast desicion. She carefully started to cut the Whale out of the Rope. The baby whale, half dead with smeares of blood all over his body, that after days of suffer finally saw freedom again, slowly started to crawl back in the ocean.
